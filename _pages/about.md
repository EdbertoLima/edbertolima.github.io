I am a Senior Researcher at [BAW Research](https://www.baw.at/baw-research.html) (Federal Agency for Water Management, Austria), working on ecohydrology and remote sensing of evapotranspiration. I hold a PhD in Forest Engineering from the Universidade Federal de Santa Maria, Brazil, and was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Hydrology and Water Management (HyWa), BOKU University, Vienna.

My research looks at how land use, soil management and nature-based solutions shape the water balance, water quality and ecosystem services of agricultural and natural landscapes under climate change.

### Current work

- **[VeSchN](https://www.baw.at/baw-research/projekte/veschn.html) - Reed evapotranspiration at Lake Neusiedl** (2026-2028): a BAW Research and Forschung Burgenland project funded by the Austrian Federal Ministry (BMLUK) and the Land Burgenland. I contribute to the remote sensing component: estimating evapotranspiration of the reed belt from Sentinel-2 imagery (OPTRAM-ET), comparing global ET products in Google Earth Engine, and evaluating satellite estimates against field measurements.
- **Topic Editor**, Frontiers Research Topic *Watershed/landscape scale ecosystem services and Land Use for Sustainable Agricultural Food Production*.

### Software

- **[Rainfall Erosivity Monitor](https://github.com/EdbertoLima/rainfall-erosivity-monitor)** (Python, Streamlit): web application that computes the EI30 rainfall erosivity index from GeoSphere Austria station data, detects erosive storms and issues colour-coded soil-erosion alerts with field survey recommendations. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24081100)
- **[QField erosion survey](https://github.com/EdbertoLima/erosion-survey-qfield)**: field guide and QGIS/QField project for mapping soil erosion by water following the DWA-M 921 guideline (German and English). With G. Liebhard, T. Brunner and P. Strauss. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18403752)
